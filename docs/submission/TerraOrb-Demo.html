NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data

TerraOrb

Your season's direction, read from space.

NASA Earth observation data turned into plain-language planting decisions for Sri Lanka's Dry Zone village tank farmers. Five apps, one data source.

Live: https://terraorb.pages.dev
Team: [TEAM NAME] · [MEMBER NAMES]

Demonstration: screenshots of the live website, 15 November 2026. Some pictures on the site were AI-generated (Google Flow, Canva) and are labelled as such.

The five ends

  • Farmer: tank water vs usual, when the rains started, a green / amber / red season plan, what to sow, weekly advice and a share card.
  • Learn: 16 picture lessons, children's games and challenges built on real data, with rewards.
  • Officer: a dashboard of all 103 tanks, NASA FIRMS fires and GRACE-FO water storage.
  • Research: the method, the backtests, the known data problems and the national NASA layers.
  • Plan: trip and field-visit planning with a live forecast and NASA POWER history.

NASA data: POWER · OPERA DSWx-HLS · HLS · MODIS NDVI, ET, LST · SMAP · GLDAS · GPM IMERG · FIRMS · GRACE-FO · NASADEM · GIBS
